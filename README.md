# MandiSaathi - AI-Powered Multilingual Market Assistant

**Bridging Languages, Empowering Local Trade**

MandiSaathi is a revolutionary AI-powered platform that transforms communication in Indian agricultural markets (mandis) by providing real-time multilingual translation, intelligent price analysis, computer vision-based vegetable recognition, voice interaction, and automated negotiation assistance. The system leverages advanced machine learning models to bridge language barriers and empower local trade through intelligent market insights.

## 🚀 Key AI-Powered Features

### 🗣️ Real-Time AI Voice Translation
- **Advanced Speech Recognition**: High-accuracy speech-to-text in 11+ Indian languages using deep learning models
- **Neural Machine Translation**: Context-aware translation with cultural nuance preservation
- **Natural Voice Synthesis**: AI-powered text-to-speech with accent adaptation
- **Conversation Memory**: Intelligent context management for seamless multilingual conversations

### 📱 AI Vision System
- **Instant Vegetable Recognition**: Computer vision-powered identification through camera scanning
- **Real-Time Object Detection**: 95%+ accuracy using custom-trained agricultural models
- **Multi-Vegetable Detection**: Intelligent prioritization of multiple objects in single frame
- **Confidence Scoring**: Uncertainty handling with alternative identification suggestions

### 💰 AI Price Intelligence
- **Real-Time Market Analysis**: Machine learning-powered price aggregation and trend analysis
- **Predictive Analytics**: Time series forecasting for price predictions and seasonal patterns
- **Location-Based Pricing**: AI-analyzed price variations across different mandis
- **Market Insights**: AI-generated recommendations for optimal timing and market selection

### 🤝 Smart AI Negotiation Assistant
- **Unfair Pricing Detection**: Statistical analysis to identify overpriced quotes
- **Automated Negotiation**: AI-powered counter-offers with cultural sensitivity
- **Strategic Communication**: Reinforcement learning-optimized negotiation strategies
- **Multi-Language Support**: Seamless negotiation across language barriers

### 📍 Intelligent Mandi Discovery
- **AI-Powered Location Services**: Smart nearby mandi discovery with distance optimization
- **Predictive Analytics**: Peak hours and crowd prediction using machine learning
- **Real-Time Status**: Live mandi operating status and arrival volume indicators
- **Navigation Intelligence**: AI-generated timing and route recommendations

## 🎯 Target Users

- **Buyers**: Farmers, retailers, and consumers seeking fair prices and clear communication
- **Vendors**: Market sellers wanting to reach customers across language barriers
- **Market Visitors**: Anyone navigating Indian agricultural markets with language challenges

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Modern web browser with camera and microphone support
- Internet connection for AI services (offline mode available for basic features)
- Location services enabled for mandi discovery

### Quick Start
1. **Open MandiSaathi** in your mobile browser
2. **Select Languages** - Choose your preferred language and the vendor's language
3. **Enable Permissions** - Allow camera and microphone access for full AI features
4. **Start Communicating** - Tap to speak or scan vegetables for instant AI assistance

### AI Features Demo
- **Voice Translation**: Tap the microphone and speak in your language
- **Camera Scanning**: Point camera at vegetables for instant AI identification and pricing
- **Price Intelligence**: Type or speak vegetable names for AI-powered market analysis
- **Smart Negotiation**: Let AI detect unfair pricing and negotiate automatically
